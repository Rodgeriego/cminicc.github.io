The Story Behind CMCC

I've always loved the Classic Mini. My first car was a Rover Mini Sprite 1275, and the obsession has stuck with me ever since. I'm now the proud owner of a 1987 Austin Mini Mayfair.

I wanted to create a club that brings together people who share that same love for these little cars — whether you've owned Minis for decades, have just bought your first one, or are somewhere in between.

CMCC is about getting the cars out of the garage, meeting other owners, driving some of the great roads around Cheshire and the Peak District, and enjoying what makes the Classic Mini so special.

It's deliberately local, friendly and informal. No matter whether your Mini is immaculate, modified, unfinished or simply earning its keep as a daily driver, you're welcome here.

Join CMCC

CMCC is a club for people who love the original Classic Mini.

Whether yours is a concours restoration, a modified Mini, a daily driver or a slightly battered weekend toy, you're welcome here.

We're based around Cheshire East and welcome owners and enthusiasts from across Cheshire and the surrounding areas.

What you can expect

🏁 Local meets & drives
🚗 Shows & events
📸 Member cars & photography
🤝 A friendly, informal community
🛣️ Some bloody good roads

CMCC is exclusively for the original Classic Mini. BMW MINI models aren't eligible for membership — not because we don't like them, but because we're keeping CMCC focused on the car that started it all.
